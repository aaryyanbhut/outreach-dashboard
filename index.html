What this app is

Outreach Dashboard is a local-first outreach operating system for SMMA owners, freelancers, agencies, and beginners who want a simple way to know exactly who to contact, who to follow up with, and which leads are becoming opportunities.

Your data is saved in your browser. You do not need an account. Use Save Backup if you want to protect your data or move it to another device.

Quick start

  1. Complete the welcome setup with your name/business, niche, and daily outreach goals.
  2. Import leads using Import CSV, Paste CSV, or Add Lead.
  3. Choose Instagram Mode or Email Mode beside the Next Action Engine.
  4. Use the Next Action Engine to know the next lead to contact.
  5. Click Open Lead to jump directly to that lead.
  6. Send the message manually on Instagram or email.
  7. Come back and click Done.
  8. When someone replies, click the right action button: Replied, Interested, Call Booked, Client, etc.

Welcome setup

The welcome screen asks for your name or business name, niche, Instagram outreach goal, email outreach goal, and sound preference.

  • Name / Business Name: Personalizes the app and top-left initials.
  • Niche: Helps organize your scripts and workspace.
  • Instagram Goal: How many Instagram outreaches you want to send per day.
  • Email Goal: How many email outreaches you want to send per day.
  • Sound Effects: Turns the subtle whoosh on or off.

Next Action Engine

This is the main brain of the app. It tells you what to do next instead of making you figure it out manually.

  • If follow-ups are due, it prioritizes follow-ups.
  • If no follow-ups are due, it shows the next new lead.
  • Open Lead jumps to the correct lead, expands it, and highlights it.
  • Done marks the current action as completed.

Instagram Mode and Email Mode

The mode switch controls what kind of outreach you are doing right now.

  • Instagram Mode: Only shows leads that have Instagram data.
  • Email Mode: Only shows leads that have email data.
  • If a lead has both Instagram and email, it can appear in both modes.
  • If you already contacted a lead on the other channel, the app shows a warning like Already contacted on Instagram.

What Done means

Done means you completed the current outreach action in the selected mode.

  • If you are in Instagram Mode, Done marks Instagram outreach as sent.
  • If you are in Email Mode, Done marks email outreach as sent.
  • The lead can still appear in the other mode if it has that contact method.
  • Done also schedules the next follow-up based on the follow-up engine.

Follow-up engine

The app automatically schedules follow-ups after outreach is marked Done.

  • Follow-up 1: after 2 days
  • Follow-up 2: after 5 days
  • Follow-up 3: after 7 days
  • Final follow-up: after 10 days

Click Refresh Follow-ups to update which follow-ups are due today.

Reply action buttons

Click a lead card to expand it. Then use the reply buttons:

  • Replied: The lead responded.
  • Interested: The lead showed interest and moves into Hot Leads.
  • Not Interested: Stops follow-ups.
  • Call Booked: Moves into Hot Leads and updates dashboard stats.
  • Client: Marks the lead as converted.
  • Lost: Stops active follow-up tracking.
  • DNC: Do Not Contact. Stops follow-ups permanently.

Dashboard metrics

  • Total Leads: All leads currently in the app.
  • Total Sent: Leads/actions marked Done.
  • Replies: Leads marked Replied.
  • Interested: Leads marked Interested.
  • Calls Booked: Leads marked Call Booked.
  • Clients: Leads marked Client.
  • Reply Rate: A simple performance indicator based on positive/reply statuses.
  • Skipped Imports: Leads skipped during import because of duplicates or missing data.

Progress bars

  • New Outreach Progress: Shows how close you are to your daily outreach goal.
  • Follow-up Progress: Shows how many due follow-ups you completed today.
  • Daily Streak: Tracks consecutive days you completed your base daily target.
  • Best Streak: Your highest streak so far.

Action tab

The Action tab is where you execute outreach.

  • Follow-ups Due: Leads that need a follow-up today.
  • New Leads: Leads available for new outreach in your selected mode.
  • Click a lead to expand it.
  • Use Done after you send outreach.
  • Use reply buttons when the lead responds.

All Leads tab

This is your full lead database. You can search, view, edit, open notes, and delete leads.

  • Edit: Change business name, website, email, Instagram, phone, channel, or notes.
  • Notes: Add reminders or context about the lead.
  • Delete: Deletes a lead using the in-app delete modal.

Bulk select and delete

Use the checkboxes next to leads to select multiple leads.

  • Selected leads glow purple.
  • A floating delete bar appears near your selection.
  • Click Delete to open a custom delete confirmation.
  • Click Clear to remove the selection.

Hot Leads tab

Hot Leads is where money-related opportunities appear.

  • Interested leads
  • Call booked leads
  • Client leads

This helps you separate real opportunities from normal cold outreach.

Scripts tab

Save outreach scripts by niche and stage.

  • Add Script: Add a new script for a niche or follow-up stage.
  • Copy: Copies the script so you can paste it into Instagram or email.
  • Edit: Update a saved script.
  • Delete: Remove a script you no longer use.

Completed tab

Shows outreach actions that have been completed, grouped by recent completion dates. Use this to review your work and track what happened today or earlier.

Skipped tab

When importing leads, the app skips bad or duplicate rows.

  • Missing name: The row had no business/name field.
  • Duplicate: The lead already existed by email, Instagram, website, or name.

Settings tab

Use Settings to update your workspace without resetting the app.

  • Name / Business Name
  • Niche
  • Instagram on/off
  • Instagram daily goal
  • Email on/off
  • Email daily goal
  • Sound effects on/off

Import CSV

Use Import CSV to upload a lead file. Recommended headers:

  • Name
  • Website
  • Email
  • Instagram
  • Phone
  • Notes
  • Channel

The app automatically tries to understand common header names like Business Name, Company, Phone Number, Instagram URL, etc.

Paste CSV

Paste CSV is useful when you copied data from another tool and do not want to upload a file. Make sure the first row contains headers.

Load Sample and Remove Sample

Load Sample adds fake sample leads so new users can understand the app instantly.

Remove Sample appears only when sample data exists and removes only sample leads.

Save Backup and Restore

This app saves in your browser, but you should still use backups.

  • Save Backup: Downloads a JSON backup containing your app data.
  • Restore: Uploads a JSON backup into the app.
  • Use backup when switching browsers, laptops, or phones.

Export CSV

Export CSV downloads your leads in spreadsheet format. It includes names, contact info, channel status, outreach status, reply status, follow-up stage, next follow-up date, and notes.

CSV is best for viewing data in Google Sheets or Excel. JSON backup is best for restoring the app exactly.

Autosave

The app autosaves into browser storage after changes, periodically, and before closing the page. The autosave badge shows the most recent save time.

Autosave protects normal work, but it does not replace backups. If someone clears browser data, local app data can be deleted.

FAQ: Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored inside your browser using local storage. It is private to that browser unless you export and share a backup file.

FAQ: Can other people see my leads?

No. If someone else opens the hosted app link, they get their own fresh version in their own browser. They cannot see your leads.

FAQ: What happens if I close the browser?

Your data should still be there when you reopen the app in the same browser. Use Save Backup regularly for extra safety.

FAQ: What if I use another laptop or phone?

Your data will not automatically appear on another device. Use Save Backup on the first device, then Restore on the second device.

FAQ: Can I host this on my domain?

Yes. Rename the file to index.html, upload it to a static host like Netlify or GitHub Pages, and connect your custom domain.

FAQ: Is this a CRM?

It is a lightweight outreach operating system. It is simpler than a full CRM, but stronger than a normal spreadsheet for daily manual outreach.

FAQ: Does it send DMs or emails automatically?

No. This app tracks and guides outreach. You still send the messages manually on Instagram or email. This helps keep the workflow simple and safer.

FAQ: What should I do every day?

  1. Open the app.
  2. Check the Next Action Engine.
  3. Complete due follow-ups first.
  4. Send new outreach until the progress bar is complete.
  5. Update reply buttons when people respond.
  6. Save a backup every few days.

FAQ: Why do I see the same lead in Instagram and Email mode?

If a lead has both Instagram and email, it can appear in both modes. The app warns you if the lead was already contacted on the other channel.

FAQ: How do duplicates work?

The app tries to skip duplicates by checking the strongest available identifier: email, Instagram, website, or name.
